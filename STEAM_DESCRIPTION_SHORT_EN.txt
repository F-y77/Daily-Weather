[h2]Mod Overview[/h2]
The Daily Weather mod automatically detects the actual weather conditions in the game and provides corresponding buffs or debuffs to players based on different weather types.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Weather Effects[/h2]

[h3]Sunny Weather[/h3]
[list]
[*] Movement speed increased by 10%
[*] Sanity recovery rate increased by 20%
[*] Work efficiency increased by 15% (gathering, chopping, mining)
[/list]

[h3]Rainy Weather[/h3]
[list]
[*] Movement speed decreased by 10%
[*] Sanity drain increased by 15%
[*] Hunger drain decreased by 10%
[/list]

[h3]Snowy Weather[/h3]
[list]
[*] Movement speed decreased by 15%
[*] Temperature loss rate increased
[*] Attack damage decreased by 10%
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Configuration Options[/h2]

[h3]Basic Settings[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Powerful Mode:[/b] When enabled, detects weather at each time phase (Day/Dusk/Night) (Default: Enabled)
[*] [b]Show Messages:[/b] Whether to display weather announcements in chat (Default: Enabled)
[/list]

[h3]Effect Settings[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Effect Count:[/b] Number of effects provided per weather event, 1-3 options (Default: 2)
[*] [b]Effect Intensity:[/b] Intensity multiplier for effects, 0.5x to 2x (Default: 1x)
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
